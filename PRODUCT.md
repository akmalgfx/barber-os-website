# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: the user rejected Framer and asked for "the best code to fit maximum creative components but in an optimized way". Chosen: Astro (static output, zero JS by default, per-component scripts) with vanilla TypeScript; no animation libraries. Ready to grow into a multi-page marketing site.

## Users

- **Primary: barbershop owners with a team.** They run chairs, staff, stock, and money on the shop floor between cuts. They buy the Elite (Shop) plan.
- **Secondary: solo barbers.** Single-chair operators who want self-scheduling and fast checkout, on the Solo plan or the free Online Booking plan.

## Product Purpose

Barber-OS ("BOS") is the operating system for a barbershop: booking, payments/POS, staff, inventory, marketing, and AI front desk in one product. Tagline in use: "Making Barbering Better For Everyone" / "Power Your Entire Business". Success on the marketing site = visitors starting a free trial or the free booking plan ("Get Started").

## Positioning

Built specifically for barbering, not a generic salon/spa booking tool. One system covers the whole shop, from the client's booking link to the cash drawer, with its own branded hardware.

## Operating Context

Used on the shop floor on tablets, phones, the BOS POS terminal and card reader, often mid-service. Clients book through a shareable link, Google "Book Now", or the shop's own branded app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed features (from the live site and product assets):
- Online booking & appointment scheduling: customizable booking page, shareable link/SmartLink, add-on services, Google Book Now.
- Integrated payments: starts at 2.2% + $0.20 per payment, online and in person; tap to pay; save card on file; tip splitting; cash tracking.
- POS: fewer clicks, owner / barber / client views, "0% Human Error. 100% Peace of Mind."
- Optimized hardware: runs on any device; BOS hardware = POS stand with printer, card terminal, barcode scanner, cash drawer.
- Inventory & product management: real-time tracking, stock ordering, supplier connection, barcode scanning, low-stock alerts.
- Clip AI: AI front desk (client-side shop assistant: rescheduling, booking) and business coach (revenue questions). Marked "New".
- Custom branded app (App Store and Google Play).
- Product Store: sell products from the booking link; Barber-OS handles fulfillment.
- No-show protection: prepay and cancellation policy.
- Also: two-way messaging, automated email/SMS reminders, blast campaigns, client management (notes, timeline), client reviews, staff management (permissions, scheduling, personalized pricing, rewards), payroll, dynamic pricing, offers & discounts, gift cards, reporting & analytics, Meta pixel.

Pricing (live site): Solo $29/mo (7-day free trial), Elite/Shop $89/mo (7-day free trial, "Most Popular"), Online Booking plan free forever. A "$599" figure appears on the live Elite card, meaning unconfirmed (likely hardware); do not use it until it's confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: BARBER-OS (wordmark), BOS for short.
- Logo: black geometric wordmark; the B carries two cyan (#22BCEE-ish) speed/blade stripes. Source: framerusercontent logo PNG (to be supplied as SVG for production).
- Product UI uses deep teal-navy chrome with cyan/blue accents (Clip AI uses navy and royal blue).
- Visual direction (user-pinned, 2026-10-27): clean, minimal, modern, using the original barber-os.com fonts and colours (Inter / Inter Display, #F8F8F8 ground, #126DFB blue, black ink, logo cyan). The user rejected a conceptual "skin fade" world with decorative interactions as "very bad". No gimmick interactions; the only interactive elements are working recreations of the real Barber-OS product UIs for each feature.
- Informed by Apple's HIG principles (clarity, simplicity, familiarity, feedback), without looking like a plain Apple site.

## Product Principles

1. The whole shop, one system: show breadth without making it feel heavy.
2. Built by and for barbering: the craft of the chair is the reference point, not "businesses".
3. Speed on the floor: fewer taps, no client wait, so every demo should feel fast.
4. Honest money: transparent pricing and fees, no invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was given; the default target is WCAG 2.2 AA, with reduced-motion support for all interactive showcases.
